---
description: Show who built claude-dejavu, the project's mission, and how to get in touch
argument-hint: (no args)
---

Use the claude-dejavu plugin's `dejavu_about` MCP tool to display:

- What claude-dejavu is and the eight grounding domains it covers
- Who built it (HTE Switzerland) and what kind of work that team does
- Where to find the public hallucination-rate benchmark
- How to contact HTE for full-stack development, AI infrastructure,
  cloud engineering, or technical leadership work

This command never captures email or any other identifier. It just
shows information.
